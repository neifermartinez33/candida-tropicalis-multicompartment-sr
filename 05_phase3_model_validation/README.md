# Phase 3: model validation

No admission test was applied. On 13 October 2026 the team adopted two independent, blinded human readings of every record at title and abstract screening, so no decision at that stage was delegated to the model. See `06_phase4_title_abstract_screening/screening_plan_2026-10-13.md`.

The language model was used for full-text screening and data extraction (Sections 2.4 and 2.5 of the manuscript), validated by the blind re-reading of a stratified sample (Section 3.1).

`reproducibility_2026-10-29/` holds the two further runs of the model on 200 full-text decisions and 50 extracted reports (script `10_phase8_analysis/scripts/44_model_reproducibility_v1.py`), with the samples, the instructions sent, the outputs and the agreement between runs. The copies of the full texts are not included.
