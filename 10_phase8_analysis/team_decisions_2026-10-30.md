# Team decisions, 30 October 2026

Taken while answering the reviewer's points on manuscript v2, after the final analysis run of 28 October 2026
and with its results known. They change the analysis dataset and will change the pooled estimates.

## D4. The susceptibility rows of the seven reports of the B08 arbitration are extracted (supersedes D2 of 2026-10-29)

D2 left without extraction the rows of 7 reports added by the arbitration of the blind sample. The team reverses
it, for three reasons:

- The re-reading of the full-text exclusions (workbook B15) adds 8 reports to the meta-analysis, so the dataset has
  to be rebuilt in any case, and leaving 7 comparable reports out of that round could not be justified.
- D2 assumed that the two multinational reports would only change which report represents a collection. That was
  established at programme level, not for each collection: R04084 (ARTEMIS, 412 isolates) and O17938 (SENTRY, 10
  isolates tested) still have to be decided as collections (AR1, AR2, workbook B03).
- The limitation it created in Section 4.7 of the manuscript, and the sentence of Section 3.1 that none of the 41
  added reports contributed to a pooled stratum, depend only on the rows not having been extracted.

D2 stays in its file as the record of what was decided on 29 October. Rows are proposed from the full text with the
frozen extraction instruction (File S1) and verified by the reviewers against the PDF, like the earlier rounds; the
proposals of this round use Claude Code 2.1.314, recorded in the log of `47_make_b16_new_rows_v1.py`. To be declared
as a decision taken after the data had been seen in Table A1 and Table S12.

D2 lists no identifiers. Read against their PDFs, the seven reports are R04084 and O17938 (multinational) and O13199,
R08533, O13212, O06680 and R06784 (7, 6, 4, 3 and 1 isolates); R06366 is the report selected for its resistance,
because its strains are described as drug-resistant Candida.

The reading contradicts the first reason of D2 for ARTEMIS: R04084 covers 2005-2007, outside `COL_O02615`
(1992-2001), so it is proposed as a new collection and not as a change of representative. O17938 (1997) lies inside
`COL_O02615`. Both still have to be decided in workbook B03.

The rows were checked on 30 October 2026 by the language model at the request of the team (workbook
`r4_b16_new_rows_v2.xlsx`, column `reviewer`, with the AR20 proposals for O06294, O10550 and O10851). A reviewer has
to confirm them before they count as verified by the reviewers.

## D5. Round 4 decisions accepted by the team (30 October 2026)

The team accepts, as proposed in workbook `r4_b16_new_rows_v2.xlsx` and in D4: AR20 applies to O06294, O10550 and
O10851 and to no other report of the workbook; R04084 is a new collection; O17938 belongs to `COL_O02615`; O15877 is
not merged with R04084. Reviewer 1 then checked workbook B16 on 30 October 2026 and signed the 15 reports and the 53
