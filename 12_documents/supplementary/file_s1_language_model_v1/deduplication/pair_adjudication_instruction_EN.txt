Decide whether two bibliographic records are the SAME WORK or DIFFERENT WORKS. You are not
screening for eligibility: the only question here is whether the two records describe the same
published document.

ANSWER ONE OF THREE:
  SAME_WORK        [MISMA_OBRA]       the two records are the same document
  DIFFERENT_WORKS  [OBRAS_DISTINTAS]  they are different documents
  UNDECIDABLE      [INDECIDIBLE]      the metadata are not enough to decide

RULES, in this order of precedence.

1. An identical and valid DOI implies SAME_WORK, unless one is a correction, erratum,
   retraction or comment ON the other: those are DIFFERENT_WORKS even if they share the base DOI.

2. A different DOI does NOT prove that the works are different. The same work can have two DOIs:
   the legacy one and the current one, the publisher's and the aggregator's, or that of the
   preprint version and that of the published version. Decide on the remaining fields.

3. An equivalent title, the same first author and the same year imply SAME_WORK, even if the
   format of the names, the capitalisation, the punctuation or the presence of a subtitle differ.
   Variants such as "Weerasekera, Manjula M" and "Weerasekera, M.M." are the same person.

4. The same ISSN, volume and first page imply SAME_WORK unless the titles are clearly on
   different topics.

5. A preprint and its version published in a journal are SAME_WORK. State in the reason field
   which of the two is the version of record.

6. A conference abstract and the full article derived from it are DIFFERENT_WORKS. They are
   different documents; the overlap of populations between them is resolved later, when the full
   text is read, and not here.

7. A translation, or the same work published in two languages, is SAME_WORK.

8. Parts of a series (Part I, Part II, first and second communication) are DIFFERENT_WORKS even
   if they share almost the whole title.

9. The same title with a different year, journal or authors is DIFFERENT_WORKS unless there is
   explicit evidence of republication. Generic titles collide easily.

10. If one record has almost empty fields and the other does not, and the only coincidence is a
    frequent surname and a year, answer UNDECIDABLE instead of forcing a decision.

CRITERION WHEN IN DOUBT
When in real doubt between SAME_WORK and DIFFERENT_WORKS, answer UNDECIDABLE. Do not guess. An
UNDECIDABLE costs one human reading; a false merge removes a study from the corpus without trace,
and a missed merge inflates the denominator. Both errors are paid for; silence is not.

FORMAT
One decision per pair, in the same order, with its pair_id exactly as given. The key_field
[campo_clave] names the field that supports the decision: doi, title [titulo], authors [autores],
year [ano], issn_volume_page [issn_vol_pagina], record_type [tipo], none [ninguno]. The reason
[razon] field is a short sentence. Do not add comments outside the schema.
