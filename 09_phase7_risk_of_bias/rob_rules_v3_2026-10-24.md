# Risk-of-bias rules v3: assessment of methodological quality (phase 7)

*Candida tropicalis* review, PROSPERO CRD420261486387 (version 2.0, 2026-10-15).
**Fixed on 2026-10-24, after the pre-drafting audit and before recomputing any judgement with them.** They supersede `rob_rules_v2_2026-10-21.md`, which is not deleted and continues to govern what does not change here: instrument (section 1), RB1, RB2, RB3 and RB7 (section 2), RB8 values (section 3), criteria not assessed (section 4), overall judgement (section 5), SA9 (section 6) and agreement (section 7). The MD5 of this file is recorded in the log of `derive_rob_judgement_v2.py`. To be read together with `10_phase8_analysis/analysis_rules_v4_2026-10-24.md`.

## 0. What changes with respect to v2, and why

1. **RB4 uses the corrected species identification** (`species_id_v3`). The v2 classifier did not read negations: 112 reports classified as "molecular" denied (5) or restricted (107) molecular confirmation. v3 reclassifies them by the method that was actually applied to all isolates, and the team confirms this in workbook B05.
2. **RB5 recognises the standards document** identified by the v4 parser in the criterion string, in addition to the extracted field.
3. **RB6 reads the version basis of each row** (analysis rules v4, AR6) and **the missing branch is written**: in v2, 32 reports fell into an unwritten branch (standard or document absent, with no inferred version).
4. **RB9 uses the agronomic management verified** in round 2 (B07) when it exists; otherwise, the phase 7 consensus. This reconciles the C5 contradictions between the consensus of 2026-10-22 and the allocation of 2026-10-23.
5. **RB8 and the editorial status of the 150 reports that enter round 2** are extracted in workbook B12 by one reviewer and one verifier (deviation 16).
6. **Correction of a statement in v2** (section 5): it is not true that no report at critical risk remained within the estimates. There were 24 main rows of pooled strata (21 reports, 437 isolates) with an undeclared version and without MICs. Rule 4 now removes them from the main analysis (analysis rules v4).

## 1. Criteria that change

- **RB4. Species identification** (JBI 6 and domain 1). Source: `species_id_v3`. **Yes**: `molecular` or `MALDI_TOF` applied to all isolates. **Unclear**: `automated`, `chromogenic`, `biochemical`, `conventional`, and molecular confirmation only in a subset of the isolates or conditional on discordant results. **No**: `not_specified`.
- **RB5. Method with standards document** (JBI 7 and domain 3). **Yes**: method declared and some standards document, either extracted (`interp_documents`) or identified in the criterion string (basis `document` or `eucast_document`). **Unclear**: method declared without a document. **No**: method absent or standard `not_reported`.
- **RB6. Interpretive criterion with document and version** (domain 4). Version bases per row (AR6):
  - declared: `document`, `year_in_string`, `revised_marker`, `eucast_document`, `eucast_year_in_string`;
  - inferred: `publication_year`, `declared_cutoff`, `eucast_unversioned`, and every row with an `inferred_from_*` flag;
  - flagged: `pre2012_doc_published_2013plus`.

  **Yes**: standard declared and all rows of the report with a declared basis, with none inferred or flagged. **Unclear**: some row inferred or flagged, or standard or document absent, and the report provides MICs that would allow reinterpretation. **No**: the same without MICs. The branch of standard or document absent without an inferred version, which was not written in v2, follows the same MIC criterion, and its count is recorded in the log (`rb6_written_branch`).
- **RB8. Declared deduplication.** Values and scoring of v2. Source: the phase 7 consensus (`rob_consensus_2026-10-22.xlsx`) or, for the new reports, workbook B12 (`dedup_rule_final` if the verifier corrected it; otherwise, `dedup_rule`). Every value other than `not_declared` requires a verbatim quote and page.
- **RB9. Documentation of the site in non-clinical compartments.** **Not applicable** to reports of C1 only. **Yes**: agronomic management documented, whether present or absent (`agronomic_management_v3` from B07, or the consensus). **Unclear**: not assessed.

## 2. Provisional mode

`derive_rob_judgement_v2.py --provisional` codes a report without RB8 as `not_retrievable` (Unclear) and a report without editorial status with the mechanical proposal (preprint, grey literature or peer-reviewed), writes this in the `provisional` column and flags the output. The FINAL run stops if any report eligible for the meta-analysis has no RB8.

## 3. Overall judgement and SA9

No changes (v2, sections 5 and 6). The judgement is computed per report and carried over to the collection through the main report of each stratum (AR2). SA9 removes the collections whose main report is rated High.

## 4. Deviations (added to deviations 11 to 15 of v2)

16. **Round-2 phase 7 with one reviewer and one verifier** for the 150 reports that enter through the verification of 2026-10-24, rather than two independent reviewers; it is declared with its number.
17. **Rules RB4, RB5, RB6 and RB9 corrected after seeing the overall judgement under v2** (58.8% High). The corrections arise from classification defects found in the audit, not from the result; the judgement under v2 remains in `rob_assessment_v1` and the difference is reported.

## 5. Outputs

- `make_rob_books_round2_v1.py`: workbook B12 (`round2_2026-10-24/r2_b12_rob_new_reports_<date-time>.xlsx`).
- `derive_rob_judgement_v2.py`: `results/rob_assessment_v2_<date-time>[_PROVISIONAL].csv`, one report per row with RB1 to RB9, judgement, reason, `dedup_rule`, `editorial_status`, and a log with MD5.
- In GRADE, the study limitations domain is based on the overall judgement and is downgraded because of its partial basis (deviations 12, 13 and 16).
